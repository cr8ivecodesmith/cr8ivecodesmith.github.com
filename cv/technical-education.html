Profile

I am a software engineer and educator. I design training around the problems people encounter and the work they want to do, drawing on my engineering experience to connect concepts with practical examples, discussion, and practice.

I have taught in professional workshops and undergraduate courses, mentored engineers, and worked with volunteers learning to lead. In each setting, I want people to understand enough to make their own decisions and continue learning beyond our time together.
