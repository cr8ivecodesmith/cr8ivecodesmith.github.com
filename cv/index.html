Overview

My work has moved from IT support and enterprise applications through web development, Python engineering, SaaS platforms, and technology leadership. Across those roles, I’ve been responsible for understanding unfamiliar systems, improving how they are built and operated, and helping colleagues make sound technical decisions.

I currently lead technology and engineering work at TotalRewards Software. Alongside that role, I co-founded CodeHappy, where I design and facilitate technical training, and PythonPH, where I help develop volunteer capability and community programs. Engineering, teaching, and volunteer leadership are different kinds of work, but each has pushed me to think more carefully about how people build understanding, exercise judgment, and take responsibility for shared outcomes.

Areas of practice

Engineering leadership and mentoring · Software architecture and technical strategy · Python web/backend systems · SaaS engineering · Developer tooling and delivery practices · Cloud infrastructure and operations · Technical curriculum design and facilitation · Community and volunteer development
